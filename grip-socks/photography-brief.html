Production Brief

Photography Direction

Every image slot on the storefront, with the shot it needs. Written for the day you replace the placeholders with your own photography.

The site currently runs on 8 stand-in images reused across ~40 slots. This brief lists what each slot actually calls for, so nothing gets missed on a shoot day.

Applies to every frame

  • Natural daylight, soft shadows, bright but never blown out
  • Clean real environments — minimal studio, modern apartment, boutique space
  • Women 25–35, natural and confident, relaxed expression
  • Socks always legible, integrated into the styling rather than staged
  • No gym atmosphere, no performance or workout intensity
  • No dramatic or coloured studio lighting, no heavy contrast
  • No teenage or exaggerated influencer styling
  • No excessive beige, no wellness-retreat mood, no cold luxury
