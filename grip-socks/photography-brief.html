Production Brief · V2

Photography Direction

Every image slot on the storefront, with purpose, scene, model direction, pose, camera, lighting, composition and a generation prompt.

The live site currently runs on 13 stand-in frames reused across ~45 slots. Filenames below are the real paths — drop a replacement at the same path and it appears with no code change.

Brand visual positioning

What every frame has to communicate

  • Modern femininity
  • Pilates lifestyle, not Pilates performance
  • Beautiful details
  • Joyful movement
  • Personal style
  • Reads like a premium women's fashion brand
  • Reads like a Pilates boutique
  • Reads like a lifestyle accessory campaign
  • Reads like clean ecommerce photography

Never

  • Gym advertisement feeling
  • Fitness competition or athletic performance
  • Yoga retreat atmosphere
  • Overly wellness-focused framing
  • Childish or teenage aesthetic
  • Excessive pink
  • Excessive cream or beige interiors
  • Dramatic light, heavy contrast, coloured light

Environment mix

70%
Real lifestyle environment — modern Pilates studio, boutique fitness space, bright architectural interior, minimal apartment
20%
Ecommerce product environment — clean wall, simple floor, minimal furniture
10%
Studio photography — product details, fabric, packaging

Colour, light, model, clothing

  • Environment colour: white, soft grey, warm ivory, natural wood
  • Accent: small amounts of muted pastel only
  • Never: large pink backgrounds, heavy beige, cream-only rooms, colourful backdrops
  • Light: natural daylight, soft shadow, clean highlights — morning window light preferred
  • Model: women 25–35, modern, elegant, natural, confident
  • Never: teenage look, overly sweet expression, influencer exaggeration
  • Clothing: black leggings, white tops, grey activewear, soft neutrals
  • The socks are a fashion detail, not sports equipment

Quality standard

Every image must pass all three

  • Product visibility — the socks are understood within 3 seconds
  • Lifestyle imagination — she can picture herself wearing them
  • Premium brand feeling — reads as a modern feminine lifestyle brand

The customer should not think "I need sports socks." She should think "This is a beautiful Pilates lifestyle item that matches who I want to be."
