Frequently asked questions

Everything you'd want to know before joining the Pilates princess life.

Ordering & Shipping

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 1–2 business days. Free shipping over $40, with tracking sent straight to your inbox.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we currently ship to the US, Canada, UK, and EU, with more countries joining soon.

Can I change my order after placing it?

Reach out within an hour of ordering and we'll do our best — just email us with your order number.

Sizing & Fit

Which size should I choose?

Most women wear S/M (US 6–8.5). Between sizes? The knit is forgiving, so size down for a snugger hug or up for extra room.

Do the socks run small or large?

True to size, with a little stretch built in for comfort.

Product Care

How do I wash them?

Machine wash cold, inside out, with your gentle things. Lay flat to dry to keep them soft.

Will the grip wear off?

No — the silicone sole is studio-tested and keeps its grip wash after wash.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

Free returns within 30 days, no questions asked — trying something pretty should always feel safe.

Can I exchange for a different color?

Of course. Start a return or exchange from your order confirmation email, or reach out and we'll help.

About Tenduu

Are these just for Pilates?

They're made for Pilates first, but plenty of our Pilates princesses wear them at home on rest days too.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes — add a gift note at checkout and we'll wrap your order in tissue with a little ribbon.
