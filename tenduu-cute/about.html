About Tenduu

Why we make cute grip socks.

Because what you wear to class should feel like you — not like generic equipment. So we design grip socks the way you'd pick out a favorite accessory: for the color, the little details, the way they make an outfit feel finished.

A grip sock has one job. We just think it can also be the cutest part of your outfit.

Why the details matter

Small design choices, on purpose

The scalloped cuff

A tiny detail most socks skip — because it's the first thing you notice when you put them on.

The dot grip sole

Studio-tested for real hold, shaped so it still looks cute peeking out mid-move.

The colorways

Each one designed to actually match your leggings, not just exist next to them.

What we're not

Just a Pilates fashion accessory

We're not here to sell you a lifestyle or a transformation. We just make one thing — grip socks — and we try to make them the prettiest, most fun version of themselves. That's the whole idea.
